airpods pro on linux

I moved to Linux, and my AirPods Pro 3 quietly became ordinary Bluetooth earbuds: no noise cancellation switch, no battery, no auto-pause. Apple was never going to fix that, so I designed the app myself — and in one night took it from a Figma frame to a working tray app with Claude Code.

Product Designer · side project
01

diegetic battery indicators

The battery isn't a widget next to the earbuds — it lives on them. A thin charge bar is painted right onto each stem, following the slant of the plastic like an LED Apple forgot to install. Green when you're fine, red below 25%, quietly grey when the AirPods are away. And when the window opens, the buds rise from below while their bars fill up.

02

two modes, zero clutter

Apple offers four listening modes and a settings maze. I kept the two people actually switch between: noise cancellation and transparency. Tap the active one again and it turns off. The AirPods also behave like they do with an iPhone: take one out and the music pauses, put both back and it resumes — and with a single bud in, noise cancellation stays on.

03

lives in the tray

Most days you never open the window. A ring around the tray icon shows the charge at a glance; one click opens a compact widget with case and earbud rings and the two modes; right-click gives a native Plasma menu.
